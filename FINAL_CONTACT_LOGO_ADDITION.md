# Final Contact Logo Addition Verification

The existing favicon mark was selected as the single professional logo for the final Contact area because it is already part of the approved Adnan Ai identity, renders locally from `/favicon.svg`, and does not introduce a new visual style or a fragile external asset dependency.

It is placed directly below `info@adnanai.com` at a restrained 32px scale with modest top spacing. Desktop and 390px mobile reviews confirm that it reads as a quiet finishing mark, keeps the email and form layout intact, adds no new section, and remains compact enough not to create unwanted whitespace.
