# Professional Review: Adnan Ai Portfolio

**Review scope:** The public site at [adnanai.com](https://adnanai.com), reviewed across desktop and mobile on 24 August 2026. No public content, layout, or code was changed as part of this review.

## Overall assessment

The website already has a **strong, premium visual identity**. The dark editorial style, cream display typography, lime emphasis, portrait-led hero, and real Website project links make it feel more considered than a generic freelancer portfolio. The best next step is not to add many sections. Instead, make the page **shorter, clearer, and more proof-led** so visitors understand your services and contact you faster.

> **Professional direction:** keep the visual design; reduce repeated messages; strengthen real proof; make the enquiry path clearer.

## What to keep

| Area | Recommendation | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| Portrait hero | Keep the large image, oversized “Adnan Ai” name, dark background, and lime accent. | It gives the website an immediate personal and premium identity. |
| Three core services | Keep only **AI Web Development**, **AI Automation**, and **AI Agents** as the main services. | The offer is focused and easy for a client to understand. |
| Real Website portfolio | Keep Aqualume and N A Metal with their live public links. | Real, clickable work is the strongest proof on the site. |
| AI Systems section | Keep it as practical workflow patterns, not claimed client case studies. | This is honest and shows what you can build without overstating experience. |
| Email and Instagram | Keep these as the public contact methods. | They are simple, direct, and match the current site setup. |
| Dark editorial design | Keep the near-black background, cream headings, graphite cards, and selective lime details. | This is the site’s strongest design signature. |

## What to remove or merge

| Priority | Item | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | Repeated services | The page explains the same three services in the early **“What Adnan Ai builds”** cards and again in the lower **WEB / FLOW / AGENT** cards. Keep the more distinctive lower cards and remove or reduce the earlier three-card block. |
| **High** | Repeated contact prompts | There is a contact call-to-action after AI Systems and another large contact section at the end. Keep the stronger final contact section; make the earlier one smaller or remove it. |
| **Medium** | “Experience” naming | The content is really your delivery method, not employment history. Rename this section and navigation label to **“Process”** or **“How I work.”** |
| **Medium** | Brand casing differences | Use **Adnan Ai** with the same capitalization everywhere. Some visible copy still uses “Adnan AI.” Consistency looks more professional. |
| **Low** | Extra decorative lime labels | Keep lime for actions, key headings, and important system labels. Reduce it in secondary decoration so the important actions feel more premium. |

## What to add — only with real information

The site should add **proof, not more generic design sections**. Do not add testimonials, client logos, project results, or numbers unless they are real and you have permission to show them.

| Priority | Addition | What you need to provide | Professional benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High** | Short “How to start” process | Your real 3 steps, for example: discovery, build, launch. | It tells a client what happens after they contact you. |
| **High** | Stronger project details | For each real project: your role, what you built, main tools, and one verified result if available. | It turns project cards into convincing case studies. |
| **Medium** | One real credibility statement | A truthful line about your focus, working style, market, or availability. | It adds trust without using fake claims. |
| **Medium** | A clear primary contact action | Use one exact button label, such as **“Discuss your project”** or **“Email Adnan Ai.”** | It gives visitors one clear next step. |
| **Later** | Booking link | A real calendar link only if you decide to use one. | Helpful for enquiries, but not needed before you have a real booking process. |

## Best structure for a more professional homepage

The recommended order is shorter and more focused:

1. **Hero:** who you are, the three services, one direct contact button.
2. **About:** short personal introduction and real location/availability.
3. **Selected work:** the two real live Website projects, with stronger project details over time.
4. **AI systems:** three clearly labelled example workflow patterns.
5. **How I work:** three concise real steps.
6. **Final contact:** email and Instagram, with one strong message and one primary action.

This structure removes repeated service and contact content while keeping all of the important information.

## Recommended first improvement

My first recommendation is to **remove the early duplicate Services cards**, retain the stronger lower **WEB / FLOW / AGENT** cards, rename **Experience** to **How I work**, and use the saved space to add a concise real **three-step client process**. This will make the page feel more confident and less repetitive without changing its premium design.

I recommend making changes only after you approve the exact text. The site should remain free of fabricated testimonials, client names, results, awards, or statistics.
