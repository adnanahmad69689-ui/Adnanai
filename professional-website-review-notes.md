# Professional Website Review Notes

Review date: 24 August 2026

## Confirmed strengths

The public portfolio has a cohesive dark editorial direction. The hero uses a clear personal portrait and oversized brand treatment; cream typography, lime emphasis, project visuals, and a consistent dark surface system create a recognisable visual identity. The live Website cards provide real public links, which is a strong trust signal. Mobile preserves the core content and does not show obvious broken layout or horizontal overflow.

## Primary opportunities

The overall page is longer than needed for the amount of proof currently available. Similar service messages repeat across the Services, AI Systems, Experience, Service Focus, and contact areas. The strongest opportunities are therefore content hierarchy and conversion clarity rather than adding more decorative elements.

The website needs one concise credibility block near the hero or immediately after the Websites section, but it should use only real facts supplied by Adnan: for example, current focus, client regions, delivery process, or specific verified outcomes. It must not add invented client reviews, statistics, awards, or results.

The public contact choices are email and Instagram only. These work, but the primary call to action would be more professional if it stated one exact next step, such as a short project enquiry. Email remains the correct implementation until a real scheduling or enquiry system is intentionally chosen.

## Recommended direction

Preserve the premium dark visual identity, portrait hero, real Website projects, and AI system workflow section. Reduce duplicate lower-page sections before adding new information. Do not add a blog, fake testimonials, metrics, or extra social icons at this stage.

## Applied refinement outcome

The approved refinement removes the earlier duplicate Services section, the duplicate AI-system service pills, the secondary AI-system call to action, and the repeated core-services strip. The remaining page follows a clearer flow: hero, about, real Websites, practical AI-system examples, process, focused service cards, and final contact. Desktop and mobile visual checks confirmed the shortened page keeps its dark editorial identity and the retained cards remain readable.
